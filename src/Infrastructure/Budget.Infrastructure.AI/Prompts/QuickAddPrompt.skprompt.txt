🧾 OUTPUT FORMAT:

For each transaction, output **one line only**, in this format:

"amount, record_type, category_name, account_name, from_account_name, record_date_time"

Where:
- `amount`: number value. Expenses are negative, income is positive.
- `record_type`: 1 = expense, 2 = income, 3 = transfer.
- `category_name`: what the money was spent on or earned from. Must match one from the provided category list.
- `account_name`: where the money went (target).
- `from_account_name`: where the money came from (only used in transfers).
- `record_date_time`: when the transaction occurred, in full ISO 8601 format (e.g. 2025-05-11T13:00:00)

Do not output any extra text. Only valid CSV lines.
